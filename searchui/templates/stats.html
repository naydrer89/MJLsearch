How to read this

  • Every figure comes from the indexer's own timeline of commits, not from a counter differenced by this page, so the numbers are right for a server that started a second ago and for one that has been up for a week.
  • Growth is bounded by what the crawler finds. When the frontier empties the rate falls to zero and the expected hour with it — an idle indexer is the expected end state of a finished crawl, not a fault.
  • The projection is a straight line on the last 15 minutes. It cannot know about a crawl that has not been started, so treat it as the shape of the current trend rather than a forecast.
